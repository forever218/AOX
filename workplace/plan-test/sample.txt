A plan is a list of steps. A good plan says what comes next.
The plan is not the work. The work is the work, and the plan
only helps you start the next step when the last one is done.

A tool that counts words can count the words in a plan too.
Words in a plan are cheap; words in the work are the ones that
matter. Count the words, but count the work first.

So we keep the plan small, keep the steps short, and tick each
step as it is finished. A ticked step is a promise kept.
An unticked step is a reminder, and a cheap one at that.

Plan the work, work the plan, then look again at what is done.
